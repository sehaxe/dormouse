06retractions

  
What is not true

  
Every entry was published here first, then measured wrong. Deleting them would be the one
    dishonest move available, so they stay next to what replaced them.

  
    
    
  • “16× from the recipe ladder”
    
      
    1.92× from the optimizer rung, measured
    
      
    The 16× was Ripple-65B's result at 4B parameters, not ours.
    • 
    
  • “3 Newton–Schulz steps are a tie”
    
      
    +0.27 BPB at [832, 3072]
    
      
    The published non-monotone result, and this repo's own “1.17e-15 from the manifold”,
        were both measured on rank-64 factors. On the dense stack the 3-step chain does not converge.
    • 
    
  • “209× less compute than GPT-2”
    
      
    53×, and only per byte
    
      
    The first figure counted GPT-2's 40 GB as 40 G positions; at ~4 bytes per BPE token
        it is 10 G.
    • 
    
  • “1.927 BPB at 98 MB”
    
      
    1.660 BPB at the same bytes
    
      
    Captured before the Muon learning-rate fix (a scheduler overwrote the group's LR
        every step).
