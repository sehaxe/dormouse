# 2026-11-09 — the 2 GB run: 95M dense at the first honest data budget

**Lane:** `port/`. **Card:** RTX 5070 Ti (sm_120). **Command:** `configs/flagship3_dense.toml`,
95 035 200 params, 12 unique blocks, `max_iter=1`, `use_tsct=false`,
`n_experts=1`, `seq_len=1024`, `batch=24`, `lr=3e-4`, `opt=mix`,
`muon_lr_mult=12`, `torch_compile=true` (static, `max-autotune-no-cudagraphs`),
`mix_plan=configs/mix_refetch.toml` (6 sources), `doc_masking=false`,
seed 1, eval on `fwedu_eval.bin` (196 608 B = 8 × 24 × 1024, offset 0).
**Checkpoints:** `checkpoints/run2gb/port.pt` every 10 000 steps.

## What it proves, and what it refutes

1. **My own fit was pessimistic by 0.047.** `L = 0.990 + 2.282·D^-0.2895`
   (fitted on the first nine points, RMSE 0.002) predicted **1.241** at 2 GB;
   the run landed 1.1940. The curve flattened later than a power law fitted on
   the first 1.1 GB assumed — a warning about extrapolating from a single
   decade of data, recorded here rather than quietly re-fitted.
2. **Data, not architecture, is the live lever.** The whole architecture matrix
   (loop / TSCT / MoE / mHC, 6 arms, 120 MB each) spanned **0.164 BPB** between
   its extreme arms. This single run bought **0.297 BPB** by feeding 16.7× more
   bytes to the *unchanged* control. That is the project's own measured ranking
   of the two knobs, not literature.
3. **The dense 12-layer control is the recipe of record.** Every alternative
   measured against it at 120 MB was neutral (+0.004 for TSCT and MoE) or worse
   (loop, +0.164); none of them earned a slot in the budget run.

## Honest gaps

- **One seed.** Seed spread on this recipe is 0.008–0.011 BPB at 120 MB; at
  2 GB it is not measured at all. The number is a point, not a mean.
- **One eval window** (196 608 B). Per-domain numbers do not exist for this
  checkpoint yet (`scripts/eval_by_domain.py` is written and was blocked by the
  card being busy; the windows are carved).
- **No LR decay** (WSD cooldown) at the end — the schedule was flat-then-linear
  over the whole run. A cooldown typically buys a few hundredths.
- The mixture's `max_epochs=4` cap was not reached (2 GB over a 2.64 GB corpus),
  so no source was repeated to exhaustion.

## What this changes in the plan

The published target was "2 GB → 1.15–1.25 (projection)". Measured: **1.194**.
The next step is therefore not another architecture sweep — it is **scale**:
the corpus holds 2.64 GB, so 2 GB is already 76 % of everything available. Two
directions, both now worth a run:
1. **all of it** (2.64 GB) at 95M — the cheap +0.03…0.05;
2. **a wider model** (130M) on the same 2.64 GB — the only way to test whether
   this box's ceiling is data or capacity, and it needs no new data.
