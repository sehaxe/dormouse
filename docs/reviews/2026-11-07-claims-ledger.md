## Method

Five shapes are extracted, inline and from table columns: `X BPB`, `N params`, `Y s/step` (or `ms/step`, `tok/s`), `Y× faster/slower`, `±Z`. Every claim records its `file:line`, value, unit, kind and the evidence the document itself names. Kinds: **measured** (a log, checkpoint or bench file is named), **computed** (arithmetic, a config or a commit is named), **cited-paper** (an arXiv id or a paper is named), **speculation** (nothing is named).

Scope is `docs/reviews/**/*.md` and `docs/research/**/*.md`; this ledger excludes itself, since its own appendix is a list of claims.

Verification is mechanical and deliberately narrow:

* **BPB** — `bpb = nats / ln 2`, recomputed from the eval line at the claimed step (JSONL `fin`/`loss`; text logs' `fin=`/`ce=`). The logged byte count is checked to be positive and is **never** a divisor; a logged `bpb` that disagrees with its own nats is reported, not trusted. Floor **0.043 BPB**.
* **params** — rebuilt from the config or the checkpoint's own `.config.toml` by the repo's counter (`dormouse info`'s `param_count`, meta device, one subprocess). Floor **2%**.
* **speed** — `batch × seq_len × grad_accum / median(tokens_per_s)` from the JSONL run, or the median `tokens_per_s` itself. Floor **10%**.
* **ratio** — a `×` comparison recomputed from two named runs; the claimed direction decides which is the denominator. Floor **10%**.
* **±** — a noise estimate; only its absence can be checked.

Status is `DERIVED` (recomputed, with the delta shown), `UNVERIFIABLE` (no artifact that can re-derive it — the missing artifact is named), or `RETRACTED` (§3.2 or a scoping audit says the number measured something else). A derived mismatch is still `DERIVED`; it goes on the risk list.
