## What changed

### Fix 1 — decay form → FLA RELEASES (`fix` commit a14153e)

- **was** (`src/lib.rs`, the `DecayFn::Softplus` arm):
  `g = -softplus(exp(A_h) · z)` — `exp(A_h)` INSIDE.
- **now**: `g = -exp(A_h) · softplus(z)` — `exp(A_h)` OUTSIDE, evaluated the
  reference's order (softplus first, then the -exp multiplier).
- Evidence: `gate.py:50` (`naive_kda_gate`: `g = -A_log.exp() *
  F.softplus(g + dt_bias)`) and independently the triton twin `gate.py:167`
  (`b_yg = -exp(b_A) * softplus(b_g)`). Two transcriptions, one original file;
  the gate is Moonshot's own supported file (`gate.py:8`).
- The two are different FUNCTIONS: they agree only at A = 0; at the crate's own
  init (A = −3, z = +1) upstream gives alpha = **0.5743**, the pre-fix branch
  gave 0.5120, and the ORDER of the two answers flips with the sign of A.
- What we compute now: alpha = exp(−exp(A_h)·softplus(z)), g ≤ 0 elementwise —
  NaN-impossible by construction (exp of a non-positive), saturation =
  underflow to alpha 0. The `a_log` clamp [−10, 20] stays (the sigmoid arm's
  overflow protection; on the softplus arm it only bounds the multiplier).
- **What we still do NOT compute**: the bounded/lower-bound variant of the
  softplus arm — upstream has no such variant (`lower_bound` in gate.py belongs
  to the K3 sigmoid twin, `naive_kda_lowerbound_gate`), and
  `gen_kda_oracle.py`'s case list keeps the §1 part of the audit (sweep,
  endpoints, A=0 control) alive for the fixed arm.

### Fix 2 — read scale → FLA's `head_k_dim**-0.5` (same commit)

- **was**: `scale = 1` threaded to `chunk_wy_forward` at both chunk call sites
  and hard-coded again inside `fused.rs`'s two dispatch arms, with the claim
  "no softmax scale in KDA" as the asserted reason.
- **now**: one published function, `pub fn fla_read_scale(head_k_dim) = head_k_dim**-0.5`
  (reached as `KdaModule::k_scale()`), and EVERY path takes it: both chunk
  arms, the fused bare-CUDA arm, the fused autodiff op, `forward_recurrent`
  and the decode prefill read (folded into `q`, FLA's own place —
  `naive.py:57`).
- Evidence: `fla/ops/kda/chunk.py:474-475` and `fused_recurrent.py:261-262` —
  both `if scale is None: scale = K ** -0.5` — and `fla/layers/kda.py:262-278`
  calls `chunk_kda` with NO `scale` argument, so the shipped layer runs at the
  default. `burn-gdn2` itself does this (`gdn2/src/module.rs:299`) — burn-kda
  overriding to 1.0 was unique in the library.
- **why no trained number moves**: the factor enters only the read
  `o = q·S` (+ the intra-chunk scores) and never the state update; the next
  thing the module does is `output()`'s RMSNorm, which is invariant to a
  constant rescale — absorbed to O(eps / mean(o²)) ≈ 1e-5. The
  *fixtures/pins* move by `K**0.5` (2.83× at K=8); the trained objective does
  not move beyond that eps floor. Arithmetic in place.
