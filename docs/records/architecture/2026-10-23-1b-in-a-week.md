# A 1B-stored model in under a week — the arithmetic, and the three gaps

**Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Question:** can this box train a real 1B in < 7 days?
**Answer:** yes, **if and only if** width is made cheap. With today's dense projections it is 15-30 days; with the three width fixes it is 2-5 days at 5-10 tok/param.

---

## 1. Where the compute actually goes (measured + arithmetic)

| model | dense MAC/token | expert MAC/token | measured tok/s |
|---|---|---|---|
| medium 105M (`d=1024`, it=3) | **24.1M** | 0.96M | **31 000** |
| b1 1.07B (`d=2048`, it=4) | **128.7M** | 1.92M | **3 500** |

Two facts that govern everything:

1. **The expert bank is nearly free.** Top-2 of 1024 experts is 1.9M MAC/token — 4% of medium's dense side. That is why parameters are almost free in this architecture and *compute* is what we pay.
2. **The dense side is `iters · (4·d² + d² + d·f)` — quadratic in `d`.** Going from `d=1024` to `d=2048` multiplies it by 5.3 (24.1 → 128.7M). **Width is the entire cost of scale.**

---

## 2. The three fixes that make width linear

| fix | what it replaces | saving at `d=2048`, it=3 |
|---|---|---|
| **latent attention** (`attn_latent_rank=256`): `d→256→d` for q/k/v/g | four `d×d` projections (4d²) | 16.8M → 3.1M |
| **GQA** (`n_kv_heads=2`): k/v at `d/2` | two of the four at full width | −1.8M |
| **low-rank `out_proj`** (`out_proj_rank=d/4`) | one `d×d` | 4.2M → 2.1M |
| **3 iterations instead of 4** | 25% of the loop | −25% on all of the above |

Resulting dense cost for a 1B-stored shape:

| config | dense MAC/token | vs medium (24.1M) | expected tok/s |
|---|---|---|---|
| `d=2048` lat256 gqa2 out512 it3 | **35.2M** | 1.46× | ~21 000 |
| `d=2560` lat256 gqa2 out640 it3 | 52.5M | 2.18× | ~14 000 |
| `d=3072` lat256 gqa2 out768 it3 | 73.2M | 3.04× | ~10 000 |
| **b1 today** (`d=2048`, dense projections, it=4) | **128.7M** | 5.3× | 3 500 |

**So a 1B-stored model with cheap width costs ~1.5× today's 105M per token — not 5.3×.** That is the whole trick: the model gets 10× bigger in stored parameters while the per-token compute barely moves, because the extra parameters live in the expert bank (cheap) and the width-dependent parts become linear.

## 3. Time to a finished 1B

| tok/s | tokens/param | total tokens | wall clock |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21 000 | 5 | 5.0B | **2.8 days** |
| 21 000 | 10 | 10.0B | 5.5 days |
| 21 000 | 20 (Chinchilla) | 20.0B | 11 days |
| 14 000 | 5 | 5.0B | 4.1 days |
| 14 000 | 10 | 10.0B | 8.3 days |

**1B in under a week = 5-10 tok/param at ≥14 000 tok/s.** That is 4-8× under Chinchilla, which is legal (our flagship runs at 55 tok/param; the overtraining rule bites at *repetition*, not at low tokens/param — 4× data reuse is the ceiling we measured).

## 4. The three gaps that are still open

| # | gap | why it matters | cost to close |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **latent/GQA/low-rank width** | the entire §2 table | agent in flight (`cheapwidth`), ~200 LOC, one A/B |
| **2** | **optimizer offload to host** (`offload_level`) | a 1B needs 6.4 GB of state; a 3B needs 19.2 GB. Host RAM is 62 GB and idle | ~150 LOC (ZeRO-1 style: shard Muon momentum to pinned host, prefetch per group) |
| **3** | **gradient accumulation** | the batch that fits at 1B is small; accumulation restores the effective batch without VRAM | ~30 LOC |

Optional, adds ~10-25%: `torch.compile` re-test (the `_banks` blocker is fixed), full-step CUDA graph (the device firewall landed), fp8 (blocked: no `_scaled_mm` backward).

## 5. What "at least like DeepSeek" means, item by item

| DeepSeek-V3 mechanism | ours | status |
|---|---|---|
| **MLA** (latent attention) | `attn_latent_rank` | **in flight** |
| fine-grained experts + shared expert | E experts + one shared rank-192 | ✅ |
| **aux-loss-free balancing** (bias, not aux loss) | LFB controller | ✅ |
| **MTP** (multi-token prediction) | `mtp_k` | ✅ implemented, tie at our scale |
| **fp8 training** | `mm_dtype=fp8` forward-only | ❌ blocked (torch has no `_scaled_mm` backward) |
| DualPipe / expert parallelism | — | ❌ single GPU |
| 671B total / 37B active | 105M / 20M active | scale |

So: **four of six** DeepSeek mechanisms are present; MLA is landing; fp8 is a PyTorch limitation, not ours.

---

## 6. What this does not fix

- **Data.** 10B tokens at 4 bytes/token is 40 GB. We have 160 GB on disk, of which 100 GB is unfiltered web. Without near-dup + quality filtering, 40 GB of raw web is worth maybe 10 GB of good text.
- **LR transfer.** No muP: a 1B run needs its own LR sweep (3-5 short runs, ~1 day).
- **Repetition ceiling.** 4 epochs is the measured limit before MoE degrades — so 160 GB is our absolute ceiling (~10 days of tokens at 21k tok/s).

**The honest plan**: fix width → offload → accumulation → design the 1B config → LR sweep on 500 steps → launch. That is **2-3 days of engineering** before the run, then **3-6 days of compute**.
